# Hand-built demonstration basket

Target: 300 mm length x 220 mm width x 300 mm clear interior height.
Current arrangement: camera on basket, RFID beside fixed printed display mount.
No more 3D printing. Handle and wheels are separate later work.

## Construction choice

Four rigid foamed-PVC signboard walls, one plywood floor, external PVC corner
trim. Use 4 mm wall sheet and a 6 mm plywood floor as a prototype starting
combination. These thicknesses are not a proven payload rating. Final floor
strength depends on the actual support plate and demonstration load.

Use rigid foamed PVC (often sold as Sunboard/Forex-type signboard), not thin
paper-faced craft foam board or loose polystyrene foam. Verify the actual
material and thickness with the seller. Plain corrugated PP is an alternative
but its glue compatibility and flexible walls need a different joining plan.

The user builds the box: no professional fabrication is assumed. Purchase
offcuts where possible. A roughly 600 x 650 mm PVC sheet accommodates two rows,
each containing one 308 x 300 wall and one 220 x 300 wall, with cutting margin.
A roughly 350 x 250 mm plywood offcut accommodates the 308 x 228 floor.

## Cut list for measured 4 mm walls and 6 mm floor

| Piece | Quantity | Cut size, mm |
|---|---:|---|
| Long front/back wall | 2 | 308 x 300 |
| Short end wall | 2 | 220 x 300 |
| Plywood floor | 1 | 308 x 228 |
| External corner trim | 4 | 300 long; about 20 x 20 angle profile |

Place the two short end walls BETWEEN the long walls. All walls stand ON TOP
of the floor. Clear interior becomes 300 x 220 x 300; exterior before applied
trim is 308 x 228 x 306. Do not include the 6 mm floor in the 300 mm wall height.

If measured wall thickness is t, preserve the same joint arrangement:
- long walls: (300 + 2t) x 300;
- short walls: 220 x 300;
- floor: (300 + 2t) x (220 + 2t).

Verify dimensions after dry assembly. Allow for real sheet-thickness variation,
cut squareness and any adhesive gap rather than promising machine precision.

## Tools and joining

- Steel ruler, square, pencil, clamps or temporary tape, cutting mat/backing,
  suitable sharp knife for PVC sheet, fine-tooth hand saw for plywood/trim,
  sandpaper and a small drill/awl for retaining holes where appropriate.
- Cut using repeated controlled passes guided by the ruler; if the selected
  thickness cannot be cut cleanly with a knife, use the appropriate fine saw.
  Sand cut edges and dry-fit before committing to glue.
- Ask for an adhesive explicitly suitable for the chosen foamed PVC and corner
  trim. Use it to bond external angle trim across both faces of each vertical
  corner; ordinary hot glue is not the sole structural connection.
- Attach floor and wall joints using adhesive approved for those substrates,
  reinforced with small external brackets/fixings or backed cable-tie loops.
  Where using ties, distribute force over trim/backing rather than relying on
  a hole in a narrow strip of soft foam PVC. Keep heads outside and ends flush.
  Avoid glue-only floor suspension: the plywood floor rests on the rigid
  moving weighing plate during use.
- The simplest stronger variant uses short fasteners through corner/bracket
  joints. This is optional under the current DIY plan, not the former printed
  snap-fit scheme. Do not silently treat ordinary adhesives as equivalent to
  a validated mechanical joint.

## Assembly sequence

1. Measure actual sheet thickness; mark and label all five panels.
2. Cut, smooth and dry-fit on a level surface. Long walls enclose short walls.
3. Measure the clear cavity and both diagonals; square it before fixing joints.
4. Fit external corner trim, then bond/retain wall-to-floor joints. Let the
   selected adhesive cure according to its instructions.
5. Locate the completed floor on the existing moving weighing plate. Any
   locating blocks or retaining connections attach only to that moving plate.
   No corner foot or bracket may connect the box to the fixed steel chassis.
6. Add a small outside reinforcement pad near the upper camera corner. Use the
   existing webcam clip if it fits, or a small strap/bracket. Keep the lens
   above rim or give it a clear optical opening. Verify view before covering.
7. Put the RFID enclosure beside the fixed display using a small plastic
   bracket; verify display-mount stiffness and antenna performance in place.
8. Route camera cable down basket wall, with strain relief on basket and a
   relaxed loop crossing to fixed electronics. No taut wire or rubbing skirt.
9. Add thin decorative wrap, edge trim and branding; keep service access and
   weighing clearance. Avoid bulky inward rim trim that reduces usable opening.
10. Tare with final empty box/camera/mounts, then test known masses at centre and
    corners and the intended demo load. Check settled readings after touching
    display/reader and after product placement. Motion or touching basket still
    affects force measurements.

The 45 x 45 x 110 central block from the rough CAD is not assumed to be the real
load-cell mounting. Preserve existing correctly functioning metal scale hardware.
Basket plus camera plus mounts plus products must fit sensor capacity/platform
limits. No handle or wheel loads attach to this box.

## Corner joint and removable camera clarification

See diy-corner-and-camera-details.png. Wall edges are square cuts: the edge of
each short wall meets the inside face of a long wall. A ready-made external
90-degree PVC angle trim bridges the outside faces for the full wall height.
Use a square or rigid right-angle jig, temporary tape/clamps and compatible
adhesive. No mitre cutting or heating/bending of wall panels is necessary.

The photographed webcam already has a clip/hinged clamp. Reinforce its mounting
location with a bonded small rigid pad, and clip the camera there. The pad stays
on the box; camera and clip detach together. Check jaw opening and grip on the
chosen rim thickness. Mark camera position/angle so remounting is repeatable.
Unplug USB and open/release the clip to remove, leaving relaxed cable routing.
Remove only during empty-basket setup/maintenance; changes to mounted mass
affect scale baseline. Tare the final empty assembly after reinstalling.

The user's pictured Lakeer A3 product is listed as 600 GSM cardboard, with no
panel thickness specified. It is not selected for the structural walls or camera
grip; it can be used for templates or cosmetic finish. Standard A3 dimensions
297 x 420 mm do not accommodate a single 308 x 300 mm long wall in this plan.
