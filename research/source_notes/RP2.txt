Abstract— In big shopping malls to purchase things in the 
shop there will be a huge crowd in holidays and the crowd will 
be more during offers and discounts period. People buy more 
things on those days. After finishing purchasing, he/she should 
go for bill counter for billing purpose. The biller will bill the 
things b y using barcodes and it is a time taking process. If it 
continuous it will lead to make long queues in the billing area. 
This research work is used to avoid this problem. To overcome 
this problem the product should have RFID tags and the 
shopping troll ey s hould have LCD screen and RFID reader. 
When customer puts some product in the troll ey means it 
automatically detects the product, product name and the 
amount will be shown on the LCD screen. If you don’t want the 
product then you will take away from the tr olley, and it will 
automatically remove amount of the product from billing. The 
trolleys (Cart) first detect whether the mode of shopping is 
online or offline. And the total amount will go to the billing 
system by Zigbee module and IOT so that it can save more time 
and avoid crowding and queues. Online shopping means it 
requires network.  
Keywords—smart shopping cart, Radio Frequency 
Identification (RFID), Internet of things (IoT), Zigbee module 
I. INTRODUCTION 
The proposed smart shopping cart is more useful, comfort 
and efficient in purchasing the goods. In this work a unique 
trolley with automatic billing system using RFID is designed. 
The main objective is to avoid crowd and to provide 
comfortable shopping for customers. The smart shopping cart 
will reduce the time of billing process. The cart having RFID 
system used to deal with the billing system. The data will be 
transferred using wireless by ZigBee Module. Every single 
trolley will have separate RFID device to check the RFID tags 
and separate trolley address. The customer will automatically 
scan the product and can see the total product amount on LCD 
Screen. This will be more useful for the malls and big 
shopping complexes, and it gives a nice and comfortable 
experience for the people, and it will lead to attract people to 
the shop again and again. 
Smart/Innovative shopping cart proposed by author

will be displayed in the LCD screen and the total amount also 
will be automatically calculated and will display in the LCD

[1-2]. But in this they can’t add or remove the product. Hence

details will be known. The details will transfer to the billing 
counter by ZigBee module, but the main disadvantage is it will

A.Problem Defination 
Now a days most people prefer to live in cities and are free 
to visit Malls, Markets, Sho pping complexes to secure more 
items. They face lots of difficulties and problems in shopping. 
When the customer has not brought money enough for 
purchasing, takes lots of disappointing moment to take away 
the product. And some shopkeepers will not give an y 
discounts provided in the product and that also want to be 
rectified. Also, for the billing it takes much time and leads to 
standing in a long queue in the crowd perhaps, it is not safe to 
standing in crowd in this pandemic situation. To solve these 
problems using this solution is identified using Universal 
Product Code (UPI) and Radio Frequency Identification 
(RFID). 
B.Problem Solution 
A buyer goes into the supermarket and takes the trolley for 
buying things. Each trolley is fixed with RFID reader device.

ESP8266: The ESP-01 WIFI module is used to get connected 
to a Wi-Fi network for internet access. The ESP01 is based on 
the ESP8266 chip is shown in Fig.3. On the GISMO board, 
the ESP-01is used as a WIFI adapter to give wireless internet 
access to the microcontroller. The hardware interface between 
the microcontroller and the ESP -01 is a serial interface. The 
Rx and TX pins of ESP -01 are connected to 9 and 8 pins of 
Arduino. These pins are not hardware, so the serial library pins 
are used to create a port. 
Fig.3 ESP Pin details 
RFID Reader: Radio Frequency Identification has become 
a hot topic around the world. RFID system delivers what bar 
codes cannot: real time information on the precise location and 
status of goods in a process flow. It does it through wireless 
transfer of data between the RFID reader and the RFID tags. 
The purpose of RFID system is to enable data to be transmitted 
by a po rtable device called a tag, which is read by an RFID 
reader and processed according to the needs of a particular 
application. It’s nothing but a type of numerical values 
presents in the tags to be scanned by the RFID reader.

communication. And it is a wireless network. Most compact 
and when compared to Wi -Fi data transfer speed is less in 
ZigBee module. Because of less cost we are taking the ZigBee 
module.

VI. CONCLUSION
This smart shopping cart make a new role in an automated 
billing system used in supermarkets, malls, etc. The billing 
data are transferred by ZigBee Module and ESP Module. The 
total bill amount will send to the main server and to the bill 
counter through the main service the designed shopping cart 
will be very useful in time management we can save lots of 
time by avoiding long queues and crowd. Want to reduce the 
size of the RFID tags. The device is also water and metal 
sensitive. The RFID reader capability is of 50tags/sec. This
